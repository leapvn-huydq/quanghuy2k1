## Expected result

Exact UI state, HTTP response code, business rule or downstream data change.

## Actual result

Observed behavior - copy actual logs/response. Do not invent evidence.

## Evidence

Attach request/response (redact tokens), screenshots, trace or synthetic logs with timestamp and run ID.

## Impact and notes

Affected roles, business risk, frequency, workaround and suggested regression coverage.

## Retest and closure

Date, build/commit, rerun result, side-effect regression and tester sign-off.
